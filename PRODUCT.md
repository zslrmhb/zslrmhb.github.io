# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The site's audience includes prospective employers and collaborators reviewing Hongbin Miao's work, and readers interested in his projects and writing.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and publishing site that brings together Hongbin Miao's background, projects, notes, and photography. Visitors should be able to find the work or writing relevant to them.

## Positioning

The site presents a personal point of view through work, notes, and photography. The approved introduction is: “I learn by making. Writing keeps track of what I find; photography helps me look again.”

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the public site to review professional work, read notes and resources, or explore the photography archive. The photo archive supports continuous chronological browsing and an image viewer with keyboard and touch navigation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep factual content, links, and features unchanged during the redesign.
- The personal introduction and section labels may be rewritten. Ask for approval before changing other copy.
- The site includes project, experience, and education information; a Markdown-based notes archive; and a continuous photography archive with an image viewer.
- GitHub and LinkedIn links and the light/dark theme toggle are existing features.
- The project uses SvelteKit and prerenders routes for static hosting.

## Brand Commitments

The site belongs to Hongbin Miao (缪鸿彬). A Living World uses the author’s photographs, original writing, and work as three paths through the same site. The approved photography experience lets visitors follow a colour into another frame and return to their original photo, selected area, and position. Use restrained typography, generous white space, and white or ink-green grounds. Photographs resolve to their original appearance. Work, Notes, and Photography remain directly accessible.

## Evidence on Hand

Project, employment, education, notes, and photography content are present in the repository. Do not invent testimonials, customer claims, or other proof that is not present.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the accuracy of the existing work and education record.
- Serve both professional visitors and readers looking for the notes.
- Keep projects, notes, and photography directly discoverable.
- The personal introduction and section labels may be rewritten. Leave all other copy unchanged unless the user approves another edit.
- Do not add unsupported claims.
