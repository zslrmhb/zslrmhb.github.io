The interface sits on a warm paper white by day and a charcoal with a hint of green at night. Day uses #faf9f6 / #242421 / #5c5b54 for ground, text, and secondary text; night uses #1b1e1d / #ecebe4 / #b5bbb5. Photographs keep their original colours in both themes. On desktop, content occupies roughly two thirds of the viewport; on smaller screens it expands with clear side gutters.

- **Home:** Ocean opens the site, with text on the left and the photograph on the right on wide screens. A point extends into the single opening rule. Type and the photograph form as the visitor scrolls, then colour samples land on the photograph. The cover links into the same frame in Photography; the author’s portrait sits with the personal introduction, beside the experience and education record. Experience, education, and projects show titles and details together with spacing and aligned rows. Notes and Photography stay accessible from the three-item navigation.
- **Photography:** Display the full archive on one continuous page, newest first. On wide screens an opening pair leads into a tighter grid, and mobile displays two photographs per row. Year links jump to the first photograph of that year within the same sequence. Keep the original aspect ratios, brief date captions, and one rule above the archive. Visual echoes remains an optional way to follow local colour, whole palettes, or contours from a photograph.
- **Notes:** An editorial list prioritizes titles and their existing descriptions. Each article opens with a title, summary, date, and return link. Keep prose centered, make room for figures, and keep the contents behind an inline disclosure.
- **Motion:** Use scroll position for the reversible point-to-line-to-plane opening only. The opening releases into normal browsing on the same scroll axis. Notes and the photo viewer display content immediately and restore the original list position on return. Show the formed cover immediately with reduced motion.
- **Controls:** Use text or a simple icon for small actions, with an underline or weight for the current choice. Keep keyboard focus visible. Pair image selection with a named keyboard control. Keep full photographs and original links available in the static page.
